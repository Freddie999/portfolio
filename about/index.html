ABOUT / THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

About me.

I’m Freddie, a geospatial software engineer building web applications, backend services, and spatial-data systems.

At Zipline, I built web application features and backend services for mapping and flight-planning workflows supporting autonomous delivery. My work enabled teams to review spatial data, edit obstacles in 3D, and validate flight routes. I also developed APIs, asynchronous processing workflows, and spatial database migrations, working across React, TypeScript, Go, Python, and PostgreSQL/PostGIS.

I own Geomersive and work on its spatial-data application, including dataset and product catalogs, upload workflows, mapping viewers, and processing workers. The main application is private and under development. The public tile utilities are a smaller code example I maintain.

I’m pursuing a Master of Urban Spatial Analytics at the University of Pennsylvania, with expected graduation in May 2027. My undergraduate degree is a BSc in Geomatic Engineering from the University of Mines and Technology.

Earlier work included GIS analysis, satellite-image interpretation, and field mapping for sanitation and agricultural projects.
